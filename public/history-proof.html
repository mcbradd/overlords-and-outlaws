At every seat: the same four Laws

Recognition of the Kindreds

At Proclaim, name two native heirs from different printed branches, distinct from your Ruler. Maintain your old Ruler and at least one named heir until succession. At succession, choose a remaining named heir. Retire the old Ruler and install that heir. Maintain the supported successor through settlement.

The Charter

At Proclaim, name a native heir and a different native Charter Witness, both distinct from your Ruler. Maintain your old Ruler, heir and Witness until succession. At succession, Retire the old Ruler and install the heir. Maintain the supported successor and the same native Witness through settlement.

The Act of Succession

At Proclaim, seal one native Outlaw as heir, distinct from your Ruler. Maintain your old Ruler and sealed heir until succession. At succession, Reveal the sealed heir, verify its native Dynasty, Retire the old Ruler and place the heir in Court as Ruler. Maintain the supported native successor through settlement.

The Marriage Settlement

At Proclaim, name a foreign heir married to your native Queen, who is not your Ruler. Maintain your old Ruler, heir and that marriage until succession. At succession, Retire the old Ruler and install the heir. Maintain the supported successor and the same marriage through settlement.

Regency

Proclaim with a native Ruler and a different native Overlord as heir. Retire the old Ruler and install the heir next round. Maintain the supported successor for two full rounds before settlement.

Entry and continued support

The primary Law route requires a native Ruler and at least three native Overlords at entry only. A failed arrangement forfeits immediately and never restores itself. Ordinary interim succession never wins.

Shared actions and timing

Every ordinary action costs one seal. Every player has three; refresh only at round start. Counterclaim uses that same supply. Pass, consent and mandatory choices are free.

ActionProcedure
BuildMove one native Outlaw into Court, ready.
WithdrawReturn your Overlord to hand. Break marriage and office; resolve succession.
PetitionDraw one Noble if the Dynasty Deck is not empty and restrictions permit.
BarterLock one or two Outlaws each. Both authorize inspection; both privately decide. Exchange and pay the initiator’s seal only on two accepts.
MarryPair your unmarried native Queen with an unpaired foreign Outlaw or unsupported foreign Overlord you control.

CounterclaimTarget controller spends a seal and Commits a matching printed-Dynasty Outlaw. Cancel the transfer. No nested response.
Address / AttackFollow the exact event. Attack rotates one ready supported person per space; contributor IDs must differ.
VeilDiscard an Outlaw and turn a never-veiled fragment face down. Unveil at start R+2. One active Veil per initiator, once ever per fragment.
ProclaimName the Law or Regency arrangement. The old Ruler Retires next round; the successor must survive its full public reign.

Round start

Rotate first seat (except round one). Unveil due fragments and check Eudoxia after each. Return Leverage. Clear petition register. Refresh three seals and ready Overlords. Conduct scheduled succession. Resolve active recurring events oldest first. Reveal N History cards sequentially, checking immediate completion after each. In first-seat order, draw one Noble if your hand has fewer than five.

Round end

Everyone passing consecutively closes the round immediately. Activate unmet pending Interregna in reveal order, after rechecking each condition. Resolve effects and expiries. Check Crown settlement after Eudoxia; begin the next round if no outcome. No reshuffle or hidden round limit.

Inheritance and physical verification

Choose N modules for N seats, N = 2–4. Shuffle their 13N Nobles and 9N History cards separately. Deal eight Nobles privately. Everyone locks exactly three and passes clockwise together; repeat with two, then one. Declare exactly three matching Nobles simultaneously. Duplicate Dynasties are legal. Appoint a Ruler; retain five Outlaws. History begins in round one.

A four-seat 2/2/2/2 failure reveals all eight. In first-seat order, take the top Noble publicly, return one different-Dynasty card to a repair packet and declare the resulting trio. Shuffle repair packets back only after all repairs. No valid two/three-seat hand can lack a trio.

Bloodline consists of native Overlords and foreign spouses supported by a native Queen in the same Court. No chains. When either spouse leaves, break both halves; a foreign survivor remains unsupported. When a Ruler leaves, choose a remaining native interim Ruler, if any.

P2: exact Crown dependencies

Before succession: supported Alba candidates; Charter heir or Witness; Habsburg foreign heir or sponsor; Regency heir. Never the sealed Tudor heir. After succession: only the same Charter Witness or Habsburg sponsor. Never target the current Ruler.

Canonical reminders

attack — Muster and Secure require two different ready Bloodline Nobles, one seal and rotation per contribution.

commit — Place an Outlaw face up in your Leverage. Return it to your hand at the next round start.

marriage — A native Queen supports only her paired foreign spouse. Losing either breaks both marriage halves.

crown — Proclaim, transfer at the next round start, then survive a full round before settlement.

bloodline — Your native Overlords and foreign Overlords married to your native Queens; no marriage chains.

Correction and reset

Pause errors at the last unambiguous state, verify retained proof, and replay legally. Revealed information cannot be made unknown. An irreparable disputed session is invalid for balance metrics. At game end reveal commitments and hands and reconcile every module ID. No card returns from The Past during play.

Five-minute public demonstration

The browser’s labeled preset uses two seats, an Alba Court with a native Queen, concealed matching claim evidence, a rival with an Alba Outlaw, P2 pending and four Alba fragments visible. Keep concealed leverage for Counterclaim or bargain it toward marriage and government. Opponents can defeat the attempt. The preset is not a guaranteed win.

Staff premise: Build a dynasty from a shared inheritance. Keep people concealed to bargain and contest, or expose them to govern. Can your government survive succession before Eudoxia completes the record?
