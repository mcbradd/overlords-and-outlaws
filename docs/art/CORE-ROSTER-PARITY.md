# Initial roster parity — 18 September 2026

The designer requires four functionally identical Dynasty sets before later thematic tuning. Every Dynasty contains thirteen ranks, with women at 6, 7, 8, 10, J, Q and men at A, 2, 3, 4, 5, 9, K. Gender is explicit identity data, independent of the old Queen role. Rank is a game allocation, not historical importance. Original source cards and portraits are preserved.

Original counts: Alba 3 female / 10 male, Plantagenet 4 / 9, Tudor 6 / 7, Habsburg 4 / 9. New count: 6 / 7 in every set. tests/core-roster.test.ts verifies every rank, not only totals.

## Artwork

Built-in image-generation tool used for seven individual portraits. Each is an artistic reconstruction, not a claimed historical likeness. Files are in public/art/core-roster/. Inspected all seven outputs: single adult subject, clear head and hands, no text or frame, period-inspired clothing, varied age/palette. Original portraits remain unchanged.

Shared prompt: Create one portrait illustration for a collectible historical card game. Subject: [below]. Painterly historical realism with fine engraved texture, natural skin, individual believable facial features, warm directional window light, restrained rich colors, quietly detailed dark background. Vertical 2:3 composition, single person framed from mid-thigh upwards, head centered near upper quarter with generous clearance above head; hands visible naturally. Full bleed rectangular artwork, no frame, no border, no rounded corners, no writing, no logos, no modern objects. The setting and likeness are an artistic reconstruction, not a copied museum painting.

## Identity references

Roster additions reflect association with a historical Court. Dynasty is a game suit, not a claim that every consort was born into the same genealogical house.

* [Yolande of Dreux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yolande_of_Dreux,_Queen_of_Scotland), Queen of Scots through marriage to Alexander III.
* [Euphemia de Ross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euphemia_de_Ross), Queen of Scots through marriage to Robert II; also [Scottish Genealogy Society](https://www.scotsgenealogy.com/Portals/0/ThemePluginPro/uploads/2023/11/23/2017_2.pdf).
* [Westminster Abbey: Matilda, wife of Henry I](https://www.westminster-abbey.org/abbey-commemorations/royals/matilda-maud-wife-of-henry-i) identifies her daughter Empress Matilda and grandson Henry II.

No historical genealogy restriction is imposed by the prototype's Marriage action: the designer's current rule is opposite gender, independent of rank or suit.
