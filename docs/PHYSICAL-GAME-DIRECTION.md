This premise applies to rules, artwork, components, interaction, research interpretation, and every future task. The user's September 15, 2026 correction is authoritative. ## Trajectory of the critiques

1. Original ambitious chamber: rich atmosphere and collectible art, but tiny floating pieces, oversized panels, overdecorated frames, imprecise alignment.
2. Competitor study: readability, state visibility, feedback, low friction and consistent interaction were useful findings.
3. Flat lane revision: simplified information but removed the intended physical identity. Explicitly rejected. Readability alone is insufficient; a web dashboard is the wrong medium metaphor.
4. First 3D synthesis: a tangible, coherent board with fixed printed cards, generous portraits, ivory typography fields, restrained engraved borders, warm metal and deep green marble. Interaction should feel like moving a component; inspection should feel like picking it up.

The logical endpoint is disciplined craft, not ever-increasing ornament or effects. Thousands of hypothetical iterations are a direction of aspiration; only executed revision passes are reported as evidence.

- Cards use a provisional 63:88 aspect ratio. Manufacturing/reference faces retain full rules. The user explicitly permits simplified battlefield faces (name, art, role, current stats) with full inspection. This is a prototype dimension, not a certified production dieline.
- Current health is the prominent live number; when damaged, show current/maximum and physical damage counters. Resting uses rotation. Marriage uses a linked pair/token. Coins, action tokens, deck/discard, crown and estates have physical counterparts.

- Portraits are artistic interpretations, with some reused House archetypes. Historical identity, dates and invented mechanics must remain distinguishable. Manufacturing art requires subsequent provenance/licensing and print-proof review.

### Implementation choices and limits

The WebGL board, cardstock bodies and pieces use Three.js. Crisp printed faces use [CSS3DRenderer](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/CSS3DRenderer.html) with the same camera and world coordinates; they are separate from the light-reactive board materials. The compositor needs a WebGL-capable browser; this pass was verified in Chrome, not every GPU/browser combination. A dense full-board view is an overview, with court cameras and inspection providing reading detail. Narrow displays pan the physical board instead of distorting card geometry.

The 63:88 card ratio and fixed face layout are a manufacturing-oriented prototype, not production print files. Physical component size, stock, bleed, ink, finish, color proofs and final art clearance still require a manufacturing pass. The September 16 portrait revision replaces the reused House archetypes with 84 individual, source-informed illustrations; uncertain faces and staging remain interpretations. These are explicit remaining limits, not reasons to abandon the physical-game premise.

### Resource-led choices and precise keywords — September 16, 2026

The designer's standing principle is to eliminate artificially imposed constraints and let tunable resources guide choices. Prefer gold costs, orders, upkeep and opportunity costs to arbitrary ceilings or once-per-turn allowances. Keep resource budgets and victory conditions explicit; do not mistake them for presentation limits. Screen size must not dictate the legal game state. Larger holdings must remain usable as physical components, with overlap only when space requires it.

Current approved mechanics: supported Commanders each grant attacking Nobles +1 Attack, including themselves; bonuses stack during attacks only. Founder entry grants 3 Stability, including setup from 9 to 12; Stability has no upper limit. Guardians enter Ready and protect all other targets while supported in court, including when Spent. Remove the response marker and once-per-rival-turn gate: Brace costs 2 gold each time, and Ambush costs 2 gold plus discarding a Conspirator from hand each time. The defender can repeat these before choosing to resolve combat. A lethal Ambush ends the attack immediately.

Card rules use functional keywords followed by italicized parenthesized reminders. Steward reads “At the start of your Readying phase, gain 1 gold.” Commander reads “Your Nobles gain +1 [the shared single-sword symbol] while attacking.” Guardian reads “Enters Ready. Your opponents may only attack your Nobles with GUARDIAN.” Do not add redundant “while in play” qualifiers. Entry triggers must retain their timing. Queen marriage is a separate paid order; Conspirator sabotage occurs on entry; Ambush explicitly discards its card. Most Nobles enter Spent and become Ready during the first phase of their owner's turn, Readying.


### Approved removal of holding limits — September 16

Remove the former limits of five Nobles in court, seven cards in hand, three Estates, 30 gold and five Shields, alongside the already removed Stability ceiling and Response allowance. Gold costs, two orders per turn, court upkeep and marriage upkeep remain tunable resources. Recalling or capturing a Noble always adds it to hand; Readying draws toward five without discarding a larger hand. Counted stacks and additional physical rows are presentation choices, never legal capacity limits. The board must expand to show larger holdings, retaining exact counts and individual Noble inspection. See [current keyword and economy rules](KEYWORD-RULES.md).
