## Scroll-surface correction, written after revision 2 failures

The parent review distinguishes active gameplay controls from deliberate reading/form surfaces. The original H05/H06/H09 checks incorrectly required the opening/form's every control to fit simultaneously, beyond the user's requirement that information being entered remain comfortably visible. Their failed rows and screenshots remain unchanged as evidence of the original stricter test; no retrospective pass is recorded.

For **opening, introduction, long references and setup forms only**, permit explicit bounded wheel/Tab scrolling, recording a capture before and after. The current field must fit fully with at least 24 CSS px of vertical context while entering text. Afterwards, a player may scroll inside the modal to Begin game, or back to Close; each control must fit and be reachable when used. Do not require entry field, submit and modal Close to remain simultaneously visible. Preserve entered values while scrolling and validate both submission and cancellation routes. For the opening, review the first screen and the visible continuation cue as a distinct visual gate, then deliberately scroll to the primary action.

**Active tutorial, target, response and confirmation controls must fit without scrolling the page or the guide to rescue them.** Never apply this form/reading exception to those controls. Safe-area margins apply when a control is used. A full-screen screenshot is still required to assess whether the allowed scroll is understandable; automatic geometry alone cannot approve the scrolling design.
