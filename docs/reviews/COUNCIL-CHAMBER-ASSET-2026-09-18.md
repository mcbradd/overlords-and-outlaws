# Council chamber environment

Built-in image-generation mode. Asset: `public/art/table/council-chamber-v1.png`. Existing original portraits are unchanged. The painted environment surrounds the separately rendered 3D table; interactive arrows use native Three.js geometry. Available creative tools were considered; additional modeling services were unnecessary for this backdrop.

Final prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Create a wide 16:9 environmental backdrop for a premium physical historical card-and-board game about competing noble Dynasties. A richly painted late-medieval council chamber viewed from a slightly elevated seated viewpoint: carved dark oak paneling, stone arches, heraldic tapestries without lettering, warm brass candle sconces near the outer edges, distant leaded windows with cool moonlight. Engraved collectible historical aesthetic, painterly realism, navy blue shadows, muted burgundy and antique gold. Broad central and lower-middle space should be very dark and calm because a real rendered 3D board and cream cards will be composited over it. Frame the edges with architecture and subtle depth; no table, no cards, no people, no text, no UI, no central ornament or bright central light. Tactile materials, atmospheric but legible, upscale historical game art. Full bleed environmental image, not a mockup.
