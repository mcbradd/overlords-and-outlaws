## Regression scenarios to write before fixes

- **FAMILY-01:** Before freezing implementation, panel members independently explain each ordinary mechanic in one short sentence plus its visible card/board example. Needing several sentences, nested exceptions or a sequence of hidden explanations reopens that mechanic's design.
- **FAMILY-02:** Audit all player-facing prompts, cards, menus and tutorial stages for sixth-grade language, consistent terms and minimal text. No multi-sentence explanation may be accepted as the fix for unclear ordinary gameplay. Automated readability metrics are supporting evidence only.
- **FAMILY-03:** A fresh screen-only learner explains the immediate move, consequence and goal without coaching or opening supplementary paragraphs. Record failures; explicitly distinguish proxy agent evidence from actual family testing.

C01 and C13 must re-evaluate the current rules under FAMILY-01 before selecting a presentation-only correction. C08 and C09 must pass FAMILY-02/03. In particular, review the succession clock, Trade's special deployment rule, Queen role versus Q rank, marriage requirements and pass/reset procedure for removable complexity. These are candidates for critique, not predetermined removals.

The following scenarios specify behavioral outcomes; tests must exercise a meaningful seam rather than copy implementation logic.

- **RENDER-01:** A first-row Court and newly occupied second row preserve each portrait and rank with contact shadows; reproduce current artifact before changing renderer parameters.
- **RENDER-02:** Played cards of different elevations, a maximal public pile and all Court rows remain visually intact during settled overview, focus, pan and reduced-motion transitions.
- **SPACE-01:** Crown, multiple attempt markers and marriage cord remain distinct from all operative card information; all public pile identities accessible.
- **FOCUS-01:** At every tutorial Watch step, compare pre-action and post-action visible scene against the taught source/target; the required resulting card is visible without unrelated input.
- **FIT-01:** Required action at 360×780 and 360×800 is fully visible and hit-testable, including its label/focus ring; repeat with badge and safe insets present.
- **KEY-01:** Type then resize the same open dialog, without navigation or reload; retain value/focus and reveal the input and submission above the keyboard boundary.
- **TRADE-01:** An exact public offer to an unknown nonempty hand never exposes whether an unrequested card is held; recipient sees both exchange identities and consistent accept/decline language.
- **TRADE-02:** Cancel, decline, accept and immediate lower-native Recruit yield their distinct conserved state and clearly different visible outcomes.
- **GUIDE-01:** Continue changes only curriculum position; legal actions alone change the board; replay/reload cannot bypass this invariant.
- **COPY-01:** Queen-role marriage allows same/adjacent ranks without A/K wrap; all active references express this rule and distinguish rank Q.
- **END-01:** Every terminal outcome shows result/restart and no next-turn instruction or active-player assertion, including the 12-round draw.
- **SAVE-01:** Broken imports produce a concise recovery message, leave existing state intact, and fit in the open form on narrow/keyboard viewports.
- **RANK-01:** All 52 identities retain the same operative rank across face canvas, reference, DOM hand and WebGL table before/after circulation and reload.
- **SUITE-01:** Every release entry targets its declared current or retained mode and reports pass/fail with exact source/served identity; no unconditional waiver or screenshot-only approval.
